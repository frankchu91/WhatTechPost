# The dev.to test — writing rubric v2 (2026-09-14)

Derived from real data: our 60 posts = 690 views / 3 reactions / 6 followers. Top posts = 100–186 reactions, 36–151 comments. Bodies of the top three (trench-coat agents, lazier-developer, 30-days-AI-code) all share the traits below. Score each draft 0–10. Ship at >= 8. Rewrite below 8.

| # | Trait | Pts | The test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contestable thesis** | 2 | One line someone would argue with. "Correct advice" = 0. If nobody could disagree, there is no post. |
| 2 | **Story opening** | 2 | First 3 lines = a specific moment that happened to me, with stakes. Not a general truth, not the reader's situation. |
| 3 | **Receipts** | 2 | Real numbers, names, failures. "9 breaks." "705 orders." "690 views." Claims without receipts = 0. |
| 4 | **Texture** | 1 | At least one human beat: self-deprecation, a flash of feeling, an aside. Varied sentence length. Not every sentence the same measured weight. |
| 5 | **Punch title** | 1 | One clause, a claim or a question, ~10 words max. No "Title. Subclause that explains." |
| 6 | **Real ending question** | 1 | Invites disagreement or a story. Not "let me know" / "follow for more." |
| 7 | **Scannable, right length** | 1 | h2 every ~150w, short paras. Discussion 400–800w, story/experiment 800–1500w, tip 400–900w. |

Safety net (must-pass, not scored): aiscan <= 2 — but bold is now allowed when structural (a rule, a key claim); the tell is bold-on-every-sentence in flat prose. Non-Meta unless author-supplied. **No fabricated experience**: every "I did X" must be something we actually did in this repo/work.

Anti-patterns that scored 0 in our 60 posts: balanced-advice thesis; opening with "Most people..." or the reader's problem; no numbers; every sentence the same polished weight; two-clause explainer titles; "if you have X I'd like to hear it" boilerplate ending.
